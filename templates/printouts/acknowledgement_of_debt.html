5. Payment Terms

  5.1. Payment may be made by cash or electronic transfer (EFT) at the Foundation's Finance Office, 207 West Road, Overport, Durban, and a receipt reflecting the allocation to the Debtor's account will be issued upon settlement of each instalment.
  5.2. Each payment will be applied to the Debtor's outstanding invoices in the order in which those debts were incurred, and recorded in the Foundation's debtors records / ledger.
  5.3. Time is of the essence of this Agreement.

6. Default

  6.1. Should the Debtor fail to pay any instalment on or before its due date, the Debtor shall be in breach of this Agreement and, at the Foundation's election (which may be made without notice), the Debt shall be in default (ipso facto).
  6.2. Upon default the full outstanding balance of the Debt shall immediately become due and payable, and interest may be charged on the overdue amount at the prescribed rate of interest as per the National Credit Act / prescribed rate of interest in South Africa.
  6.3. The Foundation may enforce the Debt through its legal advisers, and the Debtor shall be liable for all legal costs (on the attorney-and-own-client scale), collection charges and any other costs incurred by the Foundation in recovering the Debt.
  6.4. The Debtor's participation in future exhibitions and events administered by the Foundation may be withheld until the Debt is settled.

7. General

  7.1. No relaxation or indulgence granted by the Foundation shall constitute a waiver of its rights under this Agreement.
  7.2. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in respect of the Debt and no amendment shall be binding unless in writing and signed by both parties.
  7.3. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, and any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent South African courts.
